For `Дом рекламы`, IBM Plex is useful not as a standalone company case, but as a design-system signal: it demonstrates how typography, identity, and system thinking can become a durable public asset rather than a temporary visual layer.

Its relevance grows wherever design must be defended not by taste alone, but by repeatability, clarity, and structural use.
