## English

TikTok matters because it remains one of the clearest examples of how attention can be reorganized by platform design rather than by legacy media structure. For `Дом рекламы`, that makes TikTok important not only as a social product, but as a living test of how distribution power changes culture, advertising, and creative timing.

Its importance rises whenever market relevance depends less on old media order and more on algorithmic reach, creator energy, and fast-form content circulation.
