## English

This list isolates the companies that mattered most this week where AI systems, workflow systems, and public response systems began to overlap. The market is no longer reacting only to standalone models or standalone software. It is increasingly reacting to who can turn intelligence, process, and communication into one operating layer.

OpenAI led because it shaped the working logic itself. Salesforce and Microsoft followed because they connect AI to enterprise execution. HubSpot and LinkedIn mattered because response, contact, and public-facing coordination are becoming structured systems rather than loose communications habits.

- у выпуска появился отдельный срез для связки `AI + workflow + public response`;
- теперь `OpenAI` и `Salesforce` перестают быть периферийными примечаниями и становятся частью явного индексного слоя;
