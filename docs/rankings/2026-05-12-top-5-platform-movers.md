## English

This list captures the platforms whose weekly movement had the strongest effect on the communications market reading. It is not just about audience size. It is about whether platform movement changed how brands, media buyers, or communications teams had to pay attention this week.

Amazon Ads led because commerce, ad infrastructure, and platform access worked together with visible momentum. Google followed because its product and AI moves stayed structurally important. LinkedIn, Meta, and TikTok completed the list because each of them shaped public attention through platform relevance rather than through one isolated headline.

## Русский
