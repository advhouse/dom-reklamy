## English

This list isolates the companies that mattered most this week where creative tooling, workflow adoption, and public visibility worked as one layer. These are not identical tools. That is the point. Together they show how the market now rewards companies that do more than ship software. They shape habits, daily practice, and the visible operating language through which creative work gets done.

Adobe led because product depth, public clarity, and market execution aligned at once. Canva followed because it kept expanding from tool to working environment. Microsoft mattered because infrastructure became usable public workflow. Figma and OpenAI completed the list because one shapes shared design language while the other increasingly shapes shared working logic.
