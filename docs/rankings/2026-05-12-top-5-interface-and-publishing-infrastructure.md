## English

This list isolates the companies that mattered most this week as interface, delivery, and publishing systems. They do not all belong to classical advertising. That is exactly the point. For `Дом рекламы`, the market can no longer be read only through agencies, campaigns, and media owners. It also has to be read through the systems that let public products exist, move, load, publish, and scale.

Shopify led because it joins commerce, interface logic, and operational clarity in one visible stack. Cloudflare followed because distribution reliability has become part of public communications. Figma, Vercel, and Automattic stayed close because design systems, deployment systems, and publishing systems increasingly shape who can act quickly in public.
