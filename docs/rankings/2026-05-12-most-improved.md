## English

Because this is the first public week, this list is not a week-over-week rebound table. It is a momentum table. It shows which companies generated the strongest current movement inside the public rating system.

LinkedIn leads the week because its B2B advertising position, public product visibility, and platform relevance converged at the right moment. Adobe follows because its product movement was not cosmetic. It changed the practical perception of its creative and AI stack. Amazon Ads and Canva both showed strong product and distribution logic, while Reddit stood out by combining ad signal with a visible public operating trail.
