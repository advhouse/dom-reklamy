## English

This list highlights the companies whose reputation and trust position was strongest and easiest to defend this week. Reputation in this ranking is not just broad familiarity. It is the practical quality of a company’s public standing: whether it can hold credibility under scrutiny, remain readable across audiences, and avoid collapsing into noise.

Adobe led because product clarity, public systems, and market relevance reinforced one another. LinkedIn, Publicis Groupe, Google, and Canva followed because each of them combined visibility with a comparatively stable and defensible public position.
