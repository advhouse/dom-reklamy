## Public communication systems

1. LinkedIn
2. HubSpot
3. Netflix Ads
4. Spotify Advertising
5. VML

## English

This list identifies the systems that mattered most this week where public communications, audience access, and operational coordination met each other. The point is not that these five companies do the same job. The point is that they all sit close to the layer where public attention becomes structured response, targeted delivery, or coordinated campaign work.

LinkedIn led because it combines professional identity, public reach, and distribution logic. HubSpot followed because communications at scale increasingly depend on CRM discipline and repeatable contact systems. Netflix Ads and Spotify Advertising mattered because premium and audio attention now move through platformized advertising access. VML closed the list as the agency layer that tries to turn integration into execution.
