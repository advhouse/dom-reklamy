## Agency, platform commerce and market execution leaders

## English

This list isolates the companies that mattered most this week where agency force, platform commerce, and market execution began to operate as one layer. These are not identical businesses. That is the point. Together they show how the market now rewards organizations that can connect client systems, transaction surfaces, delivery logic, and real operational movement.

Amazon Ads led because commerce, distribution, and advertising infrastructure converged in one visible engine. Publicis Groupe followed because scale stayed structurally alive. Shopify mattered because commercial infrastructure became inseparable from interface and storefront execution. Monks and Dentsu Creative completed the list as agency systems trying to translate integration into market force.
