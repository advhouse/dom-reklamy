This list highlights the companies whose festival presence was strongest and easiest to read this week. Festival presence is not only about trophies. It is about how consistently a company appears inside the international awards environment through visibility, participation, creative density, and institutional relevance.

Publicis Groupe led because its presence remained structurally visible rather than episodic. WPP, Ogilvy, Dentsu Creative, and Monks followed because each of them still carries weight inside the international festival circuit, even when the market shifts toward platforms and product systems.
