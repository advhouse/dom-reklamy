## English

This list highlights the companies whose creative strength was most visible and defensible this week. Creative strength is not only about taste or recognition. It is about whether a company can turn ideas, systems, and public execution into a form that meaningfully shapes the market.

Adobe led because its creative tooling and public product movement reinforced each other. Canva, Publicis Groupe, Figma, and Monks followed because each of them connected creative capacity with visible operating relevance.
