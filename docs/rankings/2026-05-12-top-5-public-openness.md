## English

This list highlights the companies whose public trace was strongest and easiest to evaluate this week. Public openness is not a decorative value in the ranking. It is a practical advantage because it reduces fog. A company becomes easier to trust when its systems, tools, documentation, repositories, and working logic are visible in public.

Figma led because its design and system layer is unusually legible. Adobe, Reddit, Monks, and Canva followed because each of them left a visible operational trail rather than relying only on branded messaging.
